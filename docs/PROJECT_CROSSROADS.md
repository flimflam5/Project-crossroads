# Pokémon: Project Crossroads

Owner and creative director: **J Fritts**. Platform: Game Boy Advance.
Foundation: existing RHH pokeemerald-expansion 1.17.2 fork.

## Approved direction

Joe is an ordinary man in his thirties living in Olivine City, Johto. He works
in middle management for a Silph-associated subsidiary. A robbery and mistaken
criminal identification disrupt his life. His later participation in crime must
develop gradually through experiences, relationships, and consequential choices.
The tone is mature and morally ambiguous while retaining Pokémon's humor,
exploration, collecting, training, evolution, and battles.

The opening rescue gives **exactly one of Elgyem, Seedot, or Trubbish**.
The roster is curated. Johto comes first; further regions need approval and a
capacity assessment. Difficulty selection is planned; exact rules are pending.

## Canon boundary

Joe's surname, exact age, robbery participants, conspiracy, affiliations, villains,
endings, additional regions, difficulty modes, and major new mechanics are not
approved. The earlier bible incorrectly treated several of these as confirmed.
Existing previews mention Mara, Meridian, Vale, and Joseph Mercer; these remain
preserved **legacy proposals**, not newly ratified canon. The staged narrative in
`crossroads/story/opening.json` is also a proposal, not a playable quest chain.
Do not extend those proposals into permanent story content without approval.
