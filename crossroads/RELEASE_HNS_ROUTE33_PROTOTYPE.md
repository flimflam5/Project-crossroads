# Black Ledger — Route 33 prototype QA

This checkpoint adds a Heart & Soul-adapted Route 33 adjoining Azalea and Union Cave. It retains the prior map, connections and cave warp IDs, existing events and approved gameplay, and includes the previously tested Azalea Charcoal Kiln. **It is a prototype, not a completed or exact HGSS Route 33 or a completed Johto.** The current route remains 32×24; HGSS is 32×32. Its western seam, cave placement, two Apricorn objects and NPC locations still require reconstruction. Union Cave is an older foundation and its basement connections with the Ruins of Alph remain unfinished.

Game source: `5c308d1fbc66e202ee6da0c4de712079955851b0`. Rebuilt ROM SHA256: `d78f533b7cbc67d69a7eba333bc95595ba14747e1669ee3516b5e0fc070f0faf`. The fresh build reproduced the prior candidate exactly; 32 MiB padded ROM, 30,349,544-byte payload, EWRAM 226,448 bytes and IWRAM 28,392 bytes. No private saves are included.

Static generator, donor graphics, map connection, starter and Olivine checks passed. On this ROM, scripted mGBA controller tests covered the Azalea/Route 33 seam and Union Cave entry/return, 39 sampled wall approaches, four modeled ledge jumps, NPC/sign interactions, one copied QA10 old save and normal Save/cold Continue. Party, boxes, starter, flags and decoded inventory were checked. Source metatiles, palettes and actual VRAM were compared. Title, all three starters, Fly, Surf, other sampled field actions and a practice battle also passed with the disclosed fixtures. The starter test skips the inherited introduction; wild encounters were disabled in RAM for the route tour. Dive, watering, decoration and VS Seeker were not exercised. See the accompanying evidence for exact coverage.

Collision coverage is sampled; a single old-save fixture does not prove all old positions. Owner Delta playtesting and visual acceptance remain pending. The donor's art credits and source hashes are recorded under `crossroads/donor/hns-route33` and `crossroads/donor/hns_azalea_exterior`; Pokémon Heart and Soul requests credit for itself and its upstream contributors. Joe, the custom Gyms, music, starter system and accepted content remain in the source.

For Delta on iPhone: back up your existing save, save the ZIP in Files and extract it, import the `.gba`, and use a normal **Continue** rather than an older save state. Walk from Azalea east onto Route 33 and back, inspect the border, trees, ledges and cave entrance, then enter Union Cave and return. Speak to the resident and read both signs; save and reload. Try the kiln east of Azalea Gym as a regression. Report clipping, blocked exits, visual mismatches or save issues. Your review determines visual acceptance.
