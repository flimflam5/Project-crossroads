# Black Ledger — Route 33 HGSS-aligned refinement R2

Route 33 is rebuilt as a 32×32 GBA adaptation informed by HGSS and Heart & Soul, including the reference cave approach, Hiker and pink/black Apricorn trees. The existing map and warp IDs, approved functionality, Azalea seam and Joe are preserved. Apricorn dialogue is observational and grants no item; progression and rewards remain undecided.
