# Complete the current western area before expanding

Owner instruction after accepted 0.6 QA: fully flesh out the areas already present
before adding new cities or routes. Scope is Olivine City, Routes 39/38 and Ecruteak.
New rooms/floors within these locations are allowed. New regional exits stay closed.
This checklist is a development backlog, not a claim of completed content.

## Olivine

- Working baseline: seven interiors/doors, streets/piers, services, starter rescue,
  accepted title/Joe/save behavior and connection to Route 39.
- Current detail increment: distinct corporate and harbor offices; readable staff,
  freight and dispatch records; home rest/books; preserve case and travel bridges.
- Next physical landmark: recognizable lighthouse exterior, climbable floors,
  observation area, NPCs, safe stairs/returns and floor save/reload tests.
- Harbor: believable freight/loading areas, harbor-office interactions, waterfront
  population and local exploration; external passenger destinations remain closed.
- Residence/neighbors: adult living spaces and everyday interactions; keep the
  Alpha return available until its replacement is separately verified.
- Gym: finish physical layout/trainer staging; approve battle/progression rules
  before enabling badge rewards or mandatory progression barriers.

## Route 39

- Add the recognizable farm/barn/grounds within this existing map footprint,
  meaningful local interactions and more intentional landscape landmarks.
- Add approved encounters, optional trainer battles and exploration rewards.
- Preserve all three starter choices as viable and allow retreat to services.

## Route 38

- Do not connect additional routes or destinations during this completion phase.

## Ecruteak
