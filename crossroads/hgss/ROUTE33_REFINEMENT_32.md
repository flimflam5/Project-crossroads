# Route 33 — 32×32 HGSS-aligned refinement (source candidate)

This isolated candidate replaces the earlier 32×24 donor crop with a 32×32 crop of Heart & Soul's Route33_hns at (8,4), retaining the original Route 33 map/layout IDs and warp index 0. The cave door is (19,13), matching the HGSS event geometry in `canonical_events/johto-outdoor-geometry.json`. The return from Union Cave continues to target the same warp index. An [HGSS reference image](https://media.pokemoncentral.it/wiki/thumb/7/70/Percorso_33_HGSS.png/513px-Percorso_33_HGSS.png) shows the cave, nearby cliff, rain, trees and short west road. This is a two-dimensional GBA adaptation, not a claim of an exact visual match.

The city-facing seven-column buffer uses the previous map rows shifted down four tiles, preserving the Azalea boundary at the same world coordinates. With the taller route, the reciprocal offset changes from +10/−10 to +6/−6. The eight-Gym preservation validator asserts this exact and only town-event change before comparing all unrelated events to the baseline. The sign and original resident keep their scripts and event ordering; the resident is placed at HGSS (21,25). A non-battling hiker at (10,19) and two static Apricorn tree sprites at (23,26) and (24,26) represent reference objects. The trees have observational dialogue only and grant no items; their harvesting behavior is deferred to an approved progression/rewards decision. No new trainers or curated encounters are introduced.

The private secondary tileset remaps 71 donor metatiles and four donor palette banks while preserving Azalea's shared seam tile, behavior, graphic and palette. Three cells at the tree pair are adjusted to provide object placement and access; collision/elevation on other donor cells follows the converter's existing canopy safety rule. Original graphics and aggregate credit/provenance remain in `crossroads/donor/hns-route33/` and `crossroads/donor/hns_azalea_exterior/`.
